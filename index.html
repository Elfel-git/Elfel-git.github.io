Research & Publications

"Does trajectory improve peer-country selection? evidence from annual ragged-edge GDP nowcasting"

Accepted at ICEBA2026

Extracted and preprocessed ragged-edge macroeconomic data from the World Bank for 2004–2023. Developed and evaluated Level, Trajectory, and Level–Trajectory Fusion representations for peer-country selection in annual GDP nowcasting. Implemented a controlled temporal evaluation pipeline with prespecified information-availability masks, leakage-safe preprocessing, and common Ridge modeling across evaluation settings.
